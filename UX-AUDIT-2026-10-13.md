# UI/UX audit: pocketdystopia.forhuman.ca (2026-10-13)

Audited live at `d9707c9` in real Chrome over CDP, at 1440×900 (desktop), 820×1180 (tablet, touch), 390×844 (phone, touch) and 320×640 (small phone), in light and dark themes, right- and left-handed layouts. Every screen was exercised: intro, intro with a build in progress, load-code disclosure (empty, invalid), first roll, results (expanded and compressed), trait and card locks, single-trait swap, mood filter, More menu, share modal and code disclosure, pinned builds (empty, one, duplicate), keyboard shortcuts panel, clear-build confirm, home, and a crafted share link.

Severity: **P0** broken or unsafe · **P1** hurts a core task · **P2** friction or polish · **P3** nice to have. Each finding lists the solution I picked and why. Status is updated as fixes land.

## P0: broken or unsafe

### 1. A share link can run arbitrary script, and it persists
`renderCards()` interpolates `summaryText` (trait text from the decoded build) into `innerHTML` unescaped, and `escapeHtml()` does not escape quotes, so `aria-label="…"` and the pin-rename `value="…"` can be broken out of. A crafted `#build=` link with `<img src=x onerror=…>` as a hero trait executed on load (verified: it changed `document.title`). The poisoned build is then saved to localStorage, so it runs again on every later visit.

**Solution:** (a) one real escaper (`& < > " '`) used for every interpolation, text and attributes; (b) `sanitizeBuild()` validates any build that comes from outside (link, pasted code, localStorage state, pins): known sections only, exact trait counts, and every trait must be a real entry of its pool in `data.js`, otherwise the whole build is rejected; (c) new share codes carry indices into `DATA` instead of free text (finding 8), so a link can no longer carry text at all. Legacy `#build=` links still load, through the sanitiser.
**Status:** fixed

### 2. Credit footer is `position: fixed` and covers content
On results, "designed with ❤ for Human, by Nico Jan" sits over card text at every width, and the toast stacks on top of it.

**Solution:** make it an ordinary `<footer>` in the document flow: at the bottom of the intro (which shrinks its min-height to leave room) and below the cards on results.
**Status:** fixed

### 3. Card content is clipped at 500px
`.card.expanded .card-content { max-height: 500px }` drives the collapse animation. At 320px the Hero card (five traits wrapping to three or four lines) is taller than 500px and the last trait is cut off.

**Solution:** animate with `grid-template-rows: 0fr → 1fr` instead of max-height. No magic number, and it still animates.
**Status:** fixed

### 4. The Expanded/Compressed toggle does nothing on tablets
Between 768px and 1023px the toggle is shown, but CSS forces every card open and hides the chevrons, so pressing "Compressed" changes nothing.

**Solution:** collapsing works everywhere below 1024px (the same breakpoint that hides the toggle), and the chevron is shown wherever the toggle is.
**Status:** fixed

## P1: hurts a core task

### 5. On phones the Roll button is out of thumb reach, in both hands
Roll is the app's primary action and sits in the top corner. In left-handed mode it moves to the top-left, which is the hardest spot to reach for a left thumb. Mirroring the whole top bar also reverses its reading order (home ends up last).

**Solution:** on phones (≤600px) Roll moves to a fixed bottom action bar with a visible "Roll" label and Undo beside it, in the natural thumb zone. Left-handed mode mirrors the bottom bar (Roll on the left) and keeps trait/card actions on the left. The top bar stays in natural order in both modes, since the top of the screen is out of thumb reach either way. Desktop and tablet keep Roll in the top bar, now with a visible label.
**Status:** fixed

### 6. Trait actions are nearly invisible, and impossible to discover on touch
Swap and lock buttons sit at `opacity: 0.2` (#6B7280 at 20%) and only brighten on hover. Touch devices have no hover, so the per-trait lock (the core "lock what you love" mechanic) looks like decoration.

**Solution:** on `(hover: none)` devices they are always visible at a legible tertiary colour. On pointer devices they rest at a visible-but-quiet level and brighten on row hover or focus. Locked state stays amber.
**Status:** fixed

### 7. Touch targets are 36×36
Top-bar icons, card actions and trait buttons are 36px, under the 44pt HIG minimum, and the 36px buttons sit 2px apart.

**Solution:** 44px targets on coarse pointers, including trait buttons, menu items and mood options. To pay for the width on phones, trait text drops to 16px and card padding tightens.
**Status:** fixed

### 8. Share links are about 2,900 characters
The code is base64 of URI-encoded JSON of every trait string. Messaging apps truncate or mangle links that long, and the "code" is unreadable noise.

**Solution:** v2 codes pack the build as indices into the trait pools (16 slots, lock bits, mood) into about 22 bytes, which comes out as a ~30-character base64url code: `…/#b=AgAB…`. Builds that can't be expressed as indices fall back to the old format. `data.js` is now append-only (documented at the top), so old links keep resolving. The load box also accepts a full pasted link, not just a code.
**Status:** fixed

### 9. Material Symbols downloads 3.9 MB
The stylesheet requests the full variable font (all four axes, every icon). Until it arrives, icons render as their ligature names ("casino", "lock_open").

**Solution:** request only the ~30 icons used (`&icon_names=`), pin the axes to the values the CSS actually uses, and use `display=block` so names never flash.
**Status:** fixed

### 10. "Save as image" is a screenshot of the UI
html2canvas captures the card grid with every swap/lock button. There is no title or logline, there is an empty grid cell, and the result depends on viewport width (a long single column on phones). It also costs a 37 KB third-party script.

**Solution:** draw a purpose-made 1080×1350 share card on a `<canvas>`: title, logline, then the five sections, in the app's colours and fonts, with no controls and the same output on every device. html2canvas is removed.
**Status:** fixed

### 11. Locking a card doesn't lock its traits
With a card locked, each trait's swap button stays enabled and `rerollSingleTrait()` will still change the trait. Its trait lock buttons still read "Keep this".

**Solution:** inside a locked card, swap is disabled and trait locks render as held (disabled, with the tooltip "Card is locked"). `rerollSingleTrait()` refuses while the section is locked.
**Status:** fixed

### 12. The "pin" flow is indirect, and allows duplicates
The pin icon and the `P` shortcut (labelled "Pin current build") both just open the panel, where you have to find an empty "+" tile. The same build can fill all six slots, and every pin is labelled with the hero's first trait, so two pins are indistinguishable.

**Solution:** the panel gets a primary "Pin this build" button, and `P` pins straight to the first free slot with a toast. If the build is already pinned the button says "Pinned in slot N" and is disabled. Each pin shows a second line (the setting) to tell them apart. If all slots are full, replace mode opens.
**Status:** fixed

### 13. More menu runs off-screen in left-handed mode
The menu is anchored left at 240px wide, so on a 390px phone it overflows to 426px and the page scrolls sideways.

**Solution:** with the top bar back in natural order (finding 5), the menu anchors to the right edge of its button on every width, and is clamped to the viewport.
**Status:** fixed

## P2: friction and polish

### 14. Logline sentences start lowercase
"…the Age of Beige Uniforms. parkour expert (run, jump, repeat) meets chad dictator…". Trait text is written lowercase for list use, but the logline stitches it into sentences.
**Solution:** capitalise the first letter of each stitched sentence (the source data stays as it is).
**Status:** fixed

### 15. Compressed summaries always end in "..."
"talks to plants (the plants talk back)..." looks truncated even when the trait is complete, and says nothing about what's hidden.
**Solution:** show the first trait in full, then a muted "+4 more".
**Status:** fixed

### 16. Changing mood silently re-rolls the build
Picking a mood immediately replaces every unlocked trait, with nothing to say so.
**Solution:** keep the re-roll (the mood only means something if it applies) and toast "Re-rolled for Corporate dystopia. Locks kept." Undo already covers it.
**Status:** fixed

### 17. The "Undo roll" label shoves the top bar sideways
After every roll the label expands inline for 1.5s, and Share, Pin and More jump left and back.
**Solution:** in the top bar the label becomes an absolutely positioned pill under the button. It floats in the first time undo becomes available in a visit, and on keyboard focus. On phones Undo lives in the bottom bar with a permanent text label, so nothing moves.
**Status:** fixed

### 18. Toast wraps to three lines and collides with things
Positioned with `left: 50%` and shrink-to-fit, the toast is capped at half the viewport ("Locked — it'll / survive your / next roll"). It also overlaps the footer.
**Solution:** `width: max-content` up to the viewport minus margins, raised above the bottom action bar on phones.
**Status:** fixed

### 19. Desktop grid leaves a hole
Five cards in a two-column grid leave the bottom-right cell empty, and the short Setting card sits beside a tall Squad card.
**Solution:** Setting moves first as a full-width "scene" card, with WHEN and WHERE side by side on wide screens, followed by Hero | Villain and Squad | Obstacles. This matches the logline, which opens with the setting. Keyboard shortcuts 1–5, the text export and the share image follow the new order.
**Status:** fixed

### 20. iOS asks for motion permission on your first tap, wherever it lands
`initShakeDetection()` hooks the first click anywhere to `DeviceMotionEvent.requestPermission()`, so the first thing a new iOS user sees after tapping the die is a system permission prompt.
**Solution:** "Shake to roll" becomes a More menu toggle, shown only where DeviceMotion exists. On iOS the permission is requested when you switch it on. Elsewhere it defaults to on, as today.
**Status:** fixed

### 21. The load box ignores Enter, and its errors are terse
Pressing Enter in "Paste a build code..." does nothing. "Invalid build code" doesn't say what to do next.
**Solution:** Enter loads, and the error reads "That code didn't work. Paste the whole link or code exactly as it was shared."
**Status:** fixed

### 22. Loading a build can't be undone
Loading from a code or link replaced the current build with no history entry.
**Solution:** push history before any load that replaces an existing build, and say so in the toast ("Build loaded. Undo to go back.").
**Status:** fixed

### 23. The pin-delete confirm skips focus management
`confirmDeletePin()` toggles the overlay class directly, so focus isn't moved into the dialog or restored afterwards. Escape inside the pin-rename field also closes the whole panel instead of cancelling the rename.
**Solution:** route it through `openModal()`, and stop Escape propagating from the rename input.
**Status:** fixed

### 24. The whole results screen is an `aria-live` region
`#results-screen` has `aria-live="polite"`, so every re-render queues all the card text for screen readers. Changes are already announced through `#sr-announce` and the logline.
**Solution:** remove it.
**Status:** fixed

### 25. Phone top bar has no brand
Under 600px the title is hidden and a home icon stands in.
**Solution:** with Roll in the bottom bar there's room, so the title comes back (smaller) and doubles as the home button. The separate home icon goes.
**Status:** fixed

## P3: nice to have

### 26. No web app manifest
Icons exist but there's no manifest, so "Add to Home Screen" gets a generic result.
**Solution:** add `manifest.webmanifest` (name, colours, the 192/512 icons, `display: standalone`).
**Status:** fixed

### 27. Roll button is icon-only on desktop
A tiny cycling die inside a big cyan button, with no visible label.
**Solution:** add a visible "Roll" label next to the die.
**Status:** fixed

## Checked and fine

- Dark and light themes: both legible, and lock amber reads in both.
- Modal focus trap, Escape to close, focus restore on close (except finding 23).
- Keyboard shortcuts don't fire while typing in inputs or holding modifier keys.
- Reduced motion: animations are disabled and the scanline overlay is hidden.
- The mood listbox has proper roles and arrow-key navigation.
- No horizontal overflow at 320px with menus closed.

## Review round (independent code review of the fixes)

An independent review of the change set found nine more issues, all fixed before shipping: in Compressed mode any action snapped a hand-opened card shut (now remembered until the view changes); "Shake to roll" showed on desktop because `.more-menu-item { display: flex }` beat `[hidden]`; a malformed `%` escape in a legacy link threw during startup; blocked storage threw in `savePrefs()` during startup; `mood: "constructor"` passed validation; focus fell behind the pinned panel after a pin, rename or delete re-rendered it; the code's card-lock bits followed display order; legacy links could still carry arbitrary words (now pool text only); Escape on the pin-delete confirm also closed the pinned panel. Smaller follow-ups also landed: 44px trait buttons and menu rows, a fractional-width gap at the 1024px breakpoint, a wrong toast on a bad link when a saved build exists, the iOS share sheet falling back to a download, the shake re-permission turning itself off when denied, label-in-name on the title button, the reset dialog no longer duplicating its own copy in JS, empty pin slots disabled when the build is already pinned, and wrapped links loading.
